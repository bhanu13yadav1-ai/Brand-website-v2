# Next Big Update Idea — MORE DIRTY FOR HORNY GIRLS

## Positioning
A bolder 18+ fiction and fantasy discovery update for adult women and consenting couples. Keep the tone playful, confident and suggestive; avoid claiming that all women want the same things.

## Suggested feature set
1. **Sex Stories Library** — longer original chapters, multi-part stories, browse by mood, characters, setting and reading time.
2. **More Dirty for Horny Girls** — campaign tagline and a clearly labelled 18+ editorial destination; use it as creative marketing language, not a claim about users.
3. **Mood filters** — slow burn, dark romance/mystery, desi teasing, confident flirtation, couple communication, roleplay between consenting adults.
4. **Content controls** — warning labels, skip controls, adjustable suggestiveness, clear story endings, no coercive prompts.
5. **Reading list** — only if saved progress is optional and honestly described as local/un-encrypted or backed by a properly secured account service.
6. **Story cards** — title, short hook, reading time, mood and “fiction; all characters 18+” note.
7. **Writer/editor tools** — one structured content manifest for stories, tags and review status so new chapters can be added without editing huge HTML files.
8. **Discovery panel** — “Continue reading” only after history is actually stored, plus random story button that can be skipped/re-rolled.

## Product rules
- Adult-only fiction with adult characters; consent and boundaries must be central.
- Start with non-graphic sensual storytelling and user-controlled intensity.
- No real-person private sexual content, no coercion framed as romance, and no false privacy/encryption claims.
- Treat “more dirty” as a tone/content label; avoid shaming, gender stereotypes or claims that all women have the same interests.
- Research current platform policies and review/age controls before launch.

## Phase 7 vs next update
Phase 7 includes a featured landing block and four short story starters. Long chapters, story-progress storage, multi-part series and deeper preference personalisation remain next-update scope.
