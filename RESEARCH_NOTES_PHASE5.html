Phase 5 Research Notes — reviewed 21 October 2026

Clothing measurements / bra sizing

  • Frimley Health NHS Foundation Trust, “Bra Measurement” (page shows approval date 17 February 2026 and review date 1 February 2028): https://www.fhft.nhs.uk/patients-and-visitors/patient-information-library/bra-measurement/submit/7561
  • The guide describes one measurement process used by that service. It is not a universal brand/country conversion and is not adopted as a BLACKED product chart.

Anatomy and variation

  • Planned Parenthood, “Female Sexual Anatomy”: https://www.plannedparenthood.org/learn/health-and-wellness/sexual-and-reproductive-anatomy/what-are-parts-female-sexual-anatomy
  • Planned Parenthood, “Are My Vulva & Vagina Normal?”: https://www.plannedparenthood.org/learn/teens/puberty/my-vulva-and-vagina-normal
  • Key content: vulva is the external genital area; vagina is the internal canal; labia, clitoral appearance and skin tone naturally vary.

Virginity / hymen myth

  • WHO interagency statement, “Eliminating virginity-testing”: https://www.who.int/news/item/17-10-2018-interagency-statement-calls-for-the-elimination-of-virginity-testing
  • Key content: virginity is not a medical diagnosis; no physical examination can establish sexual history; hymen appearance cannot reliably prove intercourse. Do not insert objects or recommend self-exam for this purpose.

Basic hygiene and when to seek care

  • NHS, “Vaginal discharge”: https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/vaginal-discharge/
  • ACOG, “Vulvovaginal Health”: https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/vulvovaginal-health
  • ACOG, “Disorders of the Vulva: Common Causes of Vulvar Pain, Burning, and Itching”: https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/disorders-of-the-vulva-common-causes-of-vulvar-pain-burning-and-itching
  • Key content: normal discharge varies; notable change in colour/odour/texture, persistent pain/itching/burning, sores or unusual bleeding may warrant clinical advice. Do not diagnose or delay care using the website.

Limitations

  • These pages are reputable health/education sources, but not all are India-specific. The website offers general educational information, not diagnosis or medical care.
  • BLACKED does not yet have a verified product-specific size chart; the tool therefore validates and summarizes measurements but does not predict size.
  • The research supports the text only; it does not validate product claims, images, inventory or commercial rights.
