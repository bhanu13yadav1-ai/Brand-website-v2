# Phase 6 — Image rights & new-media checklist

## Rights are per asset and per use
For each photo or artwork, record its exact path/source, creator or supplier, acquisition date, license / written permission, permitted commercial uses, attribution terms, territory, distribution channels, edit/cropping restrictions, expiry/review date and the reviewer. Keep model releases and brand/logo approvals where applicable. A publicly reachable URL or a campaign brief is not proof of usage rights.

## Before publishing a campaign
- Confirm the asset's origin (owner-supplied, licensed, generated, stock, or third-party reference).
- Obtain permission for the actual intended use: website, organic social, paid advertisements, packaging, marketplace listings, etc.
- Check whether model releases, age verification, exclusivity, attribution, retouching restrictions or culturally specific approvals apply.
- Keep any uncertain image labelled `rights not verified`, remove it from commercial campaign placements, and use a cleared replacement.
- Keep religious/cultural collection previews clearly editorial/coming-soon; don't infer a model's faith, claim community-wide representation, or imply institutional endorsement.

## Assets created for this package
Four non-photographic, original SVG diagrams are included: measurement basics, product dimension labels, consent/comfort steps, and an image-rights workflow. They are instructional diagrams, not proof of a real garment/product's measurements and not substitutes for product-specific labels or clinical advice. The register includes seed rows for them, but final owner publishing approval should still be recorded.
