Identity and measurements

The reference cards intentionally do not guess anyone’s identity, performer/stage name, fame status, penis size, or any other anatomical measurement from an image. Those are not reliably established by a photo. Only add a person’s public name or documented measurement if you have an authoritative source and are allowed to publish that information.

Public-use rights

The images were supplied by the user for this project, but that alone does not establish commercial/public distribution rights. Confirm the relevant licence or permission before publishing the site publicly. The 18+ section uses a self-attestation gate; it is not formal age verification.
