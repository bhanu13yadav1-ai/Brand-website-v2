Clear basics & when to seek help

How to measure

Keep the tape level

  1. Use a soft tape and stand naturally; do not pull so tight that it compresses the skin.
  2. Measure bust around the fullest part and underbust directly beneath the breasts.
  3. Measure the natural waist and fullest part of hips for briefs/bodysuits.
  4. Repeat once to check consistency and use the exact product’s size chart.

Normal variation

There is no single normal look

Vulvas, labia, clitoral hoods and skin tones vary. The vulva is external; the vagina is the internal canal. Appearance alone does not prove health, sexual history or worth.

When to seek care

Ask a qualified clinician

Seek medical advice for persistent pain or itching, sores, unusual bleeding, pain when urinating, or discharge that changes noticeably in colour, smell or texture. Severe pain, heavy bleeding, fever or rapidly worsening symptoms may need urgent assessment.

This site cannot diagnose symptoms. A qualified clinician can assess what is happening.

Part B · Desi Hinglish body & toy guide

Chut, land, gand — seedhi baat

Desi slang ko simple medical meaning ke saath samjho. Yeh educational guide hai, size ranking ya personal recommendation nahi.

18+ · Education
01 · CHUT

“Chut” — vulva aur vagina ek cheez nahi

“Chut” desi gaali/slang hai, koi medical measurement nahi. Vulva bahar ka hissa hai—labia, clitoris aur openings; vagina andar ka stretchy canal hai. Dono ek cheez nahi. Koi ek fixed “chut size” sabke liye normal nahi hota; body-to-body variation natural hai.

Yeh wheel vagina ki width ya depth measure nahi karta aur “best size” nahi batata. Dard, unusual bleeding ya symptoms rehne par qualified clinician se baat karo.

02 · LAND

“Land” — penis ka slang

“Land” desi bol-chal ka slang hai; medical word penis hai. Length aur girth har body mein alag hoti hain. Ek number se pleasure, compatibility ya “performance” decide nahi hoti. Online photo, joke ya wheel result se kisi ka real size guess nahi kiya ja sakta.

Real-life mein seedha, bina pressure ke baat karo. Size label medical category nahi hai.

03 · GAND

“Gand” — buttocks; “anus” opening hai

“Gand” desi slang hai, aksar buttocks ke liye bola jaata hai; anus us area ka opening hai. “Gand size” ka koi simple universal number nahi hota. Anal-use toy ke liye actual widest diameter, usable/insertable length, shape aur manufacturer instructions check karo—sirf S/M/L label pe bharosa mat karo.

Anal toys ke liye flared base ya safe retrieval stop zaroori hai, taaki toy andar slip na kare.

04 · DILDO

Dildo ka size kaise samjhein?

Overall length poore toy ki lambai hai; insertable length woh hissa hai jo use ke liye bana hai; widest diameter sabse chaudi width hai. In teenon ko alag-alag dekho. “Regular” ya “XL” har brand mein same nahi hota—actual dimensions aur material-care instructions padho.

Aaram, safai, body-safe material aur toy kis use ke liye bana hai—yeh sab important hain. Koi range sabke liye “best” nahi hoti.

05 · BUTT PLUG

Gand ke toy ke liye safety rules

Gand/anal use ke liye sirf woh toy choose karo jo isi use ke liye bana ho aur jisme flared base ya safe retrieval stop ho. Suitable lubricant use karo; anus apna lubricant nahi banata. Chhota option bhi sabke liye automatically safe nahi—fit, design, material aur instructions matter karte hain.

Sharp pain, force ya bleeding ko ignore mat karo. Stop karo; severe/persistent pain or bleeding mein medical help lo.

06 · NO PRESSURE

Dirty talk alag; real-life decision alag

“Aaj land ya dildo?” poll sirf fictional mood pick hai. Poll, wheel ya story real-life consent nahi hai. Har adult ko yes, no, pause ya skip bolne ka full right hai. Body ya toy size ko lekar sharminda karna zaroori nahi.

Privacy & local storage

Your data, explained

This is a front-end prototype. It has no login, backend account, analytics endpoint, real checkout or server-side profile in this version.

  • Wishlist, demo bag, fit notes and (only after opt-in) story bookmarks/progress can be stored in this browser’s local storage. They are not encrypted; other people using this browser profile may be able to see them.
  • Measurements are saved only after you opt in. They remain in this browser and are not synced or encrypted.
  • The optional photo preview uses a temporary browser object URL and is not uploaded by this page.
  • Campaign images are loaded from public raw-GitHub URLs. The image host receives a normal image request, not your saved notes.
  • The 18+ prompt is self-attestation, not formal age verification.

Use the clear-data button only when you are ready to remove this app’s saved values from this browser. Other websites’ storage is not touched.
